Our story

Meet Melanie

At the heart of Stella James is the creative voice and founder, Melanie Bronfman. She lends her vision and business savvy to Stella James, as well as the names of her beloved children Stella (age 5) and James (age 7).

The brand was born of a desire to keep a symbol of her cherished children with her at all times in a way that felt both authentic and meaningful. She envisioned letter-shaped diamonds that could be worn as staple jewelry but was suprised to find no existing options that satisfied her desire for both premium design and heirloom quality.

She set out to create a collection of precisely cut, tasteful diamond letters at an attainable price point and the shining result of her journey is Stella James.

“This brand began with a feeling I couldn’t put into words—the kind that lives deep in the heart, so I created something to wear instead. I hope this piece carries a part of your story, just as it carries mine.”
With all my heart,Melanie Bronfman

Craftsmanship

01

Never Mined

We use lab-grown diamonds because we believe beauty should never come at the expense of ethics or the environment. Created using advanced technology that replicates the natural diamond-growing process, lab-grown diamonds offer the same brilliance, structure, and durability as mined stones, without the ecological disruption or concerns associated with sourcing. It’s a more responsible way to honor what matters most: the people you love and the world they’ll inherit.

02

The Art of the Cut

Each Love Letters pendant begins with a single, lab-grown diamond, selected for its exceptional clarity and color, then hand-cut into its distinctive letter form. This artisanal process, done entirely by hand, ensures that no two pendants are ever exactly alike. By working with lab-grown diamonds, we maintain the highest standards of beauty and brilliance while honoring our commitment to ethical craftsmanship.

03

Golden Setting

Every pendant is cast in solid 18K gold, offered in your choice of classic yellow or luminous white, and finished with meticulous attention to detail. The gold setting is designed not only to cradle each diamond securely, but to enhance its form, elevate its sparkle, and endure as a modern heirloom. This is fine jewelry made to last a lifetime, and to carry your story with grace.

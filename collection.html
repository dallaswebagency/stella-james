Love Letters

Your story, spelled in diamonds. Each initial in the Love Letters collection is hand-carved from a single lab-grown diamond and set in 18k gold, designed to honor the people and moments that matter most. Available in two sizes: 7.5mm and 9mm. These pendants can be worn solo or strung together to create a name, a word, or a quiet declaration of love.

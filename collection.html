How long will my order take?

All orders are made to order and shipped with insured, trackable delivery. Please allow 4–6 weeks for handcrafted production.

Can I return or exchange my piece?

Due to the personalized nature of our pieces, all sales are final and we cannot accept returns or exchanges. We recommend reviewing your order details carefully before completing your purchase.

What diamonds do you use?

We use lab-grown diamonds because we believe beauty should never come at the expense of ethics or the environment. Every diamond is individually selected for exceptional quality—D–E color, VS1–VVS clarity—with carat weights ranging from 0.8 to 3.5 depending on the form and size of the letter.

Which sizes are available?

Love Letters pendants are offered in two refined sizes: 7.5mm (~0.3”) and 9mm (~0.35”).

How many letters can I wear together?

These pendants are made to wear solo, layered, or linked together to tell your story. We recommend up to four letters for a harmonious, graceful look.

Yellow or white gold?

Every pendant is cast in solid 18K gold, offered in your choice of classic yellow or luminous white, and finished with meticulous attention to detail.

How do I care for my jewelry?

Store it in a soft pouch or lined box to prevent scratches. Clean gently with warm water, mild soap, and a soft brush, then dry with a lint-free cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals, ultrasonic cleaners, and exposure to extreme heat.

Can someone help me choose?

Yes. We’re here to help you find your perfect combination, whether selecting your first letter, curating a meaningful set, or creating a piece that tells your story. Chat with a specialist on WhatsApp.

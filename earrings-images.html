The image standard for every earring

Applying this to all 20 earrings makes the collection read as one curated line.

Frame

4:5 portrait, 1600 × 2000 px, the same as the pendant pages. WebP for the site, JPG for marketplaces.

Backdrop

One soft ivory (#F5F4F1) for every packshot, with the same contact shadow. No mixed denim, velvet or grey.

Scale

Product fills about 60% of the frame height, centred. Studs and hoops look the same size in the grid.

Image set

Front packshot, angle, diamond close-up, two model shots with zoom inset, and a navy brand card.

Watermark

Small Stella James wordmark at the bottom, low opacity, so the piece stays the hero. A clean version without the mark is kept for Google Shopping.

Naming and alt text

stella-james-heart-hoop-front.jpg, with alt text like “Heart Hoop earring, heart-shaped diamond in 18k yellow gold”.

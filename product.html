Each Love Letters pendant is cut by hand and crafted in radiant 18K yellow or white gold, then meticulously hand-set with lab-grown diamonds. Every diamond is individually selected for exceptional quality—D–E color, VS1–VVS clarity—with carat weights ranging from 0.8 to 3.5 depending on the form and size of the letter.

Offered in two refined sizes—7.5mm (~0.3”) and 9mm (~0.35”)—these pendants are made to wear solo, layered, or linked together to tell your story. We recommend up to four letters for a harmonious, graceful look. For help creating your perfect combination, our jewelry specialists are here to guide you. Choose to purchase your pendant on its own, or pair it with your preferred chain.

Product care

To preserve the beauty of your 18k gold and lab-grown diamond jewelry, store it in a soft pouch or lined box to prevent scratches. Clean gently with warm water, mild soap, and a soft brush, then dry with a lint-free cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals, ultrasonic cleaners, and exposure to extreme heat, as these can affect both the gold’s finish and the diamond’s setting. Remove your jewelry during activities that may cause impact to ensure it remains as brilliant and secure as the day it was made.

How to style it

It’s a story you wear.

Your Love Letters pendant is more than jewelry. Whether you choose a single initial or a collection of letters, each arrangement carries its own meaning and charm. Designed for versatility, these pieces transition effortlessly from everyday wear to special occasions.

One Letter

Keep it minimal and powerful with a single initial - your own, or someone who means the world to you. Best styled on the Stella chain for a clean, refined look that pairs easily with other fine jewelry.

Two Letters

Celebrate a connection by wearing two initials side by side - a subtle nod to a partner, child, or loved one. The bead chain adds texture and movement, giving this pairing a warm, personal feel.

Three Letters

Tell a fuller story by combining three initials - your family, children, or a trio of people closest to your heart. The choker chain creates a bold, close to the heart statement that feels both rich and timeless.
